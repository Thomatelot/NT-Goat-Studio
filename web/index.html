Difficulty

Opponents get stronger as the story stage rises: Rookie decks are the duelist's own cards with basic support, Duelist decks add the first staples, Expert decks add the full Goat staple suite, and Goat Max decks are complete Goat Format decks built around the duelist.

Boss duelists

A boss steps up one tier from stage 6 and plays Goat Max in the final stretch (stages 8 and 9).
